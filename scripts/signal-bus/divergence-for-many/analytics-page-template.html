What this is, and isn't, evidence of

＋The confluence-hold-rate relationship is real at the p<0.001 level, tested at the correct unit of analysis (zones, not touches — a zone's touches aren't independent observations of it).
－One indicator, one asset, one exchange proxy. Nothing here has been tested on ETH, on a second data source, or against realistic trading costs.
－Descriptive and tested is not the same as tradeable. No position sizing, no capacity check, no cost model — the same gap that had to be closed before the SuperTrend backtests meant anything practically.
－The 4+ confluence bucket is thin and its hold rate can swing sharply on small samples — read it as directional, not precise.
